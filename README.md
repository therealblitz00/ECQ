# Case Study III — Millennium BCP: Daily Account Balance Forecasting

Forecast each client's daily account balance for the **next 30 days**, evaluated by **MAPE** (see `problem-description.md`).

## Data
| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `data/raw/small-dataset.csv` | 50 accounts × 1095 days (the first 50 accounts of the medium dataset) |
| `data/raw/medium-dataset.csv` | 500 accounts × 1095 days |

- Wide format: one row per `ACCOUNT_ID`, one column per day (raw labels 2020-01-02 → 2022-12-31). The data is fictitious.
- The final evaluation uses a **large dataset** (not yet available; about 20× the medium one, ~10 000 accounts). All code must scale to it (vectorised, memory-aware) and run on the group's ThinkStation PGX (ARM64 Linux).
- The final forecasts are for the 30 days after the end of the data, for the same accounts.

## Evaluation
- **Official MAPE:** each day contributes |A − F| / (|A| + €1). The organisers do not specify how zeros are handled; the €1 offset keeps every day defined. The ranking of the baselines does not depend on the offset (Sprint 1, D9b).
- **Companion measures:** median per-account MAPE and MAPE on |A| ≥ €100; WAPE is reported but does not decide.
- **Decision rule:** a model is accepted only if it improves the official MAPE without getting worse on the two companion measures (Sprint 1, §17). Results are also reported without the single largest contributor of each fold.
- **Validation:** rolling origin, 5 folds of 30 days (January 2022 and the last four windows of 2022).

## Key findings

### Sprint 1 (medium dataset)
- **Calendar fix:** until 2022-08-06 each column holds the previous day's close; 2022-08-05 holds the sum of two closes. Both repaired; missing values (~1%) are all leading (accounts opened later), no imputation.
- **MAPE risk:** 22% of balances negative, 17% within €100 of zero, 358/500 accounts change sign. Overdrafts are short (median 8 days).
- **Calendar:** activity on business days only; inflows on the 8th and on the last business days of the month, outflows on days 1–5 (saw-tooth month).
- **Pensions:** 40 accounts (8%) are paid on the exact date (the 8th, or the next business day at weekends), with July and December at ~2× an ordinary month. January 2023: inflow on Monday 9 with an ordinary amount.
- **Dynamics:** random walk day to day, strong mean reversion over a month, level shifts (half the accounts changed level in the last ~2 months); differences stationary; seasonal differencing over-differences (calendar regressors instead); accounts independent of each other.
- **Not found:** no savings accounts, no single-use or abandoned accounts, no rent peak on the 8th, no credit-limit floors.
- **Segments:** four rule-based segments (near-zero/overdraft 39%, stable positive 28%, volatile positive 33%, dormant 0.2%). The CV threshold between stable and volatile is fragile and should be tuned by forecasts.
- **Clustering (check only):** 18 hand-made features, PCA to 80% of the variance (5 components), k-means → k = 2 ("persistent, calm" vs "erratic, crossing zero"). Robust to feature sets, scalings, algorithms and tsfresh; the rule-based segments explain more forecast difficulty (0.52 vs 0.34).
- **Benchmark (MAPE with €1):** naive 736%, 30-day median 2 075%; oracle best constant 43%. Most of the error is choosing the wrong level for near-zero accounts.

### Sprint 2 (medium dataset, computed before the €1 offset — to be refreshed on re-run)
- Forecasting zero scores just under 100% on every MAPE and no constant level beats it on near-zero accounts; a calendar profile gated by sign agreement gets below it.
- Stable accounts: ARIMA (weekly season) beats the last value once the largest contributor is left out. Volatile accounts: a global LightGBM with a MAPE-weighted loss beats every classical model. tsfresh features did not help.
- Segment pipeline: accepted against the zero forecast, not against the last value on the typical-account measure; the per-account choice in the near-zero group was measured and rejected.

All results are saved as CSV in `data/sprint1/` and `data/sprint2/` (start with `00_index.csv`).

## Deliverables & Tooling
- Each sprint is one self-contained Jupyter notebook (`notebooks/NN_name.ipynb`) that runs with only the CSV of balances (`DATA_PATH` in the first cell).
- Python 3.13; pinned dependencies in `requirements.txt`.

