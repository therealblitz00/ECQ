# Phase 4: evaluation report

Generated by `src/phase4_eval.py run` (2026-10-12). Every number is measured on the
**test half** of the models (5,292 queries); weights are tuned on the other half (5,263 queries).
The split is fixed (models shuffled with seed 0), so runs are comparable.

## Metrics

| Metric | Family | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `hit@4`, `hit@10` | catalogue | share of queries with a "known similar" item (same family › sub-family, theme and colour, another model) in the top 4 / 10; 2,317 test queries have one |
| `same_*` | catalogue | share of the top 4 with the same family, sub-family, colour (`CLR_DES`), finishing, material (pairs where both have a value) |
| `visual@4` | visual | share of the top 4 among the query's 20 nearest photos (queries and candidates with a trusted photo) |
| `sales_corr` | commercial | Spearman correlation of the query's sales percentile with the mean of its top 4 (sales are not used by the engine) |
| `price_ratio` | – | median of higher / lower price in the top 4 (1.00 = same price) |
| `catalogue` | | mean of `hit@4`, `same_subfamily`, `same_colour`, `same_finishing`, `same_material` |
| `objective` | | mean of `catalogue`, `visual` (= `visual@4`) and `commercial` (= `sales_corr`) |

Each family is circular for one block: `catalogue` rewards the categorical and text blocks, `visual` rewards the
image block (with only the image block, `visual@4` is 100% by definition). A search on the objective alone moves
almost all the weight to the image and loses a third of the material agreement, so the tuning below never trades
one family for another, and the image weight is left to the human review.

## Weight tuning

Greedy search on the tuning half: each block weight in turn is multiplied by 0, 0.5, 1.5, 2.
A change is kept only if the objective rises by at least 0.005 **and** none of `hit@4`, `same_subfamily`, `same_colour`, `same_finishing`, `same_material`, `visual@4`, `sales_corr`
drops by more than 0.01.

| step | change | catalogue | visual | commercial | objective |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | start | 80% | 39% | 0.625 | 0.605 |

| weights | image | categorical | text | composition | palette | numeric |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| current | 0.360 | 0.360 | 0.080 | 0.080 | 0.040 | 0.080 |
| chosen | 0.360 | 0.360 | 0.080 | 0.080 | 0.040 | 0.080 |

The current weights already come from an earlier tuning (phase4_eval.py run --apply, 2026-10-12). No change passed the rule, so the current weights are kept. The weights file was not changed (`--apply` writes it).
