# Phase 4: interactive feedback report

Generated by `src/phase4_feedback.py fit` (2026-10-21) from
138 sorted queries (1 André, 2 Pedro Correia, 3 Pedro Meireles, 4 Manuel, 5 Zé).

The reviewers drag each query's candidates into *Similar* (best first) or *Not similar*. Every similar item beats the
items below it and every "not" item; the six block weights are fitted to those preferences with a Bradley-Terry
model, pulled towards the starting weights (prior sd 0.5 on the log-weights). Candidates come from a fixed pool
(top 5 at image weights 0, 0.3, 0.6 + 2 random), shown shuffled and
blind, so the learned weights never change what a reviewer sees.

## Reviewer agreement

On the 10 queries every member sorts: 2371 candidate pairs ordered by two members,
70% in the same order. This is the ceiling: the weights cannot agree with "the team" more than
its members agree with each other.

## Applied

Not the learned weights themselves: a blend, **25% of the way** from the Phase 4 tuned weights to the learned ones
(2026-10-21), written to `src/phase3_weights.json`:
image 0.42, categorical 0.33, text 0.08, composition 0.07, palette 0.03, numeric 0.07.

| Blend | Image | Held-out preferences right | Same material (top 4) | Sales correlation | Price ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0% (earlier tuning) | 0.36 | 72.8% | 74.5% | 0.62 | 1.00 |
| **25% (applied)** | 0.43 | 73.8% | 73.9% | 0.62 | 1.00 |
| 50% | 0.49 | 73.4% | 72.1% | 0.61 | 1.00 |
| 75% | 0.55 | 74.2% | 69.7% | 0.60 | 1.07 |
| 100% (learned) | 0.62 | 75.3% | 65.6% | 0.59 | 1.08 |

Why: the fully learned weights order the most held-out preferences right, but cost 9 points of material agreement and
move the prices of the matches apart. The gaps between 73% and 75% are small for 699 held-out pairs, while the
material drop is not, so the blend keeps most of the human gain at almost no cost (proxy metrics on the test half of the
models, `phase4_eval.py run`). André's last 2 queries were not in yet (138 of 140 answers).
