# Phase 5b: the sales model against the buyer

Generated by `src/phase5b_buyer.py run` (2026-10-27). Same protocol and metrics as `forecast_report.md` (whole-life units on 241; 242 only for the same-month ranking); 95% intervals resample models (300 resamples). Buyer decisions are a benchmark here, never a feature of the delivered model.

**The buyer's reference** = median sales of the training products with the same category and the same decision. Within a category it is the buyer's decision itself, with its levels ordered by the training season (median log units, 232):

- `PUR_BET`: MX 9.68, SB 9.14, M1 8.64, M2 8.19, M3 7.82
- `TYPOLOGY`: CBA 8.3, DCBA 8.28, BA 7.99, A 6.55

**Caveat.** Purchase depth and store tiers also *cause* sales (more stock and more stores sell more), so the buyer's numbers are an optimistic reference; the model only sees the product.
