# Phase 5b: CatBoost against LightGBM

Generated by `src/phase5b_catboost.py run` (2026-11-08). Same data, splits, features, targets and metrics as `forecast_report.md`; intervals are 95% bootstrap over models (1000 resamples). Nothing of the delivered estimates is changed.

## Protocol

- CatBoost `RMSE`, 800 trees, fixed seed, 4 threads, no early stopping. The categorical attributes are native `cat_features` (missing = its own level).
- Small grid (depth 4/6/8 x learning rate 0.03/0.08 x L2 3/10) chosen on the **choice split only** (sales: first half of the new 241 models, mean absolute log error; price: 232 -> 241, mean absolute error in euros). The reported split is scored once with the chosen setting. LightGBM keeps the parameters of the forecast module (not tuned here), so the grid slightly favours CatBoost.
- Chosen: sales {'depth': 6, 'learning_rate': 0.03, 'l2_leaf_reg': 10.0}; price {'depth': 8, 'learning_rate': 0.08, 'l2_leaf_reg': 3.0}.
- Same variants as the forecast: with and without the similar past products as a feature; the price model estimates the markup over the landed cost and falls back to a model without cost.
