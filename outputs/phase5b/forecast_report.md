# Phase 5b: explainable sales and price estimates

Generated by `src/phase5b_forecast.py run` (2026-10-27). Trained on seasons 232 and 241; every number below is measured on season 242, on models never sold before (new products). Intervals: 95% bootstrap over models.

## The data the decisions rest on

- **Price:** 0 missing, 0 at 0 € or less; 40 list prices from 3.99 € to 250.00 €. Every product has a usable price.
- **Landed cost** (`PRICE_LCP`): missing for 168 colourways. Not used (see the reference row).
- **Sales:** 370 colourways have no sales row and 9 have 0 units: unknown, so left out of training and evaluation (they still get an estimate).
- **Time on sale:** median units by season {'232': 3377.0, '241': 3637.0, '242': 1336.0}; inside a season and category, later launches sold less (median Spearman of units vs days on sale +0.26). Hence the `days_on_sale` feature and the temporal test.

## How the explanations are computed

- **Gradient boosting** (LightGBM): SHAP values by TreeSHAP (`pred_contrib`). They are exact for the model (no sampling), add up to the estimate, and cost milliseconds per product, so every product gets them.
- **Similar past products**: the estimate is their similarity-weighted mean, so the products themselves are the explanation (keys listed per product in `data/processed/forecast.parquet`).
- Features are grouped into families (photo components, description components, materials, attributes…) before reporting, because single principal components have no business meaning.
