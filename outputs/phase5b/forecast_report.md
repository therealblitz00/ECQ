# Phase 5b: explainable sales and price estimates

Generated by `src/phase5b_forecast.py run` (2026-10-27). Products are colourways; tests use only models never sold in the training seasons (new products); intervals are 95% bootstrap over models.

## Protocol

- **Validation (choices): train 232 → test 241.** Which model and which features are chosen here.
- **Test (reported once): train 232 + 241 → test 242**, with the choices of the validation.
- **Time:** the planned store date (`STORE_DATE_PLANNED`, known when ordering) gives the launch month and the days on sale; the final date is revised for 56% of the products. The seasons overlap in time and the training sales are accumulated until the extraction, so they include months of the test season: a limit of the data (no dated sales), stated rather than corrected.
- **Left out:** buyer decisions (`PUR_BET`, `TYPOLOGY`) and the landed cost (reference rows only); sales are never a feature; the price model never sees the price or the numeric similarity block.

- **Price:** 0 missing, 0 at 0 € or less; 40 list prices from 3.99 € to 250.00 €.

## The delivered estimates (`data/processed/forecast.parquet`)

Never in-sample: products with known sales or price get a **cross-validated** estimate (5 folds grouped by model, so neither the product nor its other colours were seen), products without sales a forecast from all known products. Each row carries its kind, the three biggest SHAP effects, the similar past products and the **typical error measured on the test season**: sales × 3.1 either way (exp of the mean absolute log error), price ± 3.41 €. With accumulated sales only (no stores, no weeks), the sales estimate is a ranking aid for comparing new products, not an order-quantity forecast.

## How the explanations are computed

- **Gradient boosting** (LightGBM): exact TreeSHAP values (`pred_contrib`), adding up to the estimate, milliseconds per product; grouped into business families because single principal components mean nothing.
- **Similar past products**: their similarity-weighted mean, so the products themselves are the explanation. Caveat: the engine's weights were tuned in Phase 4 with an objective that includes the sales correlation, so this reference is slightly optimistic; the chosen model does not use the engine.
