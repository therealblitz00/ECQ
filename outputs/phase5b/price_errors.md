# Largest price errors on the official price test

Generated by `src/phase5b_price_errors.py` on 2026-11-08. Read-only: it reruns the price
model of `phase5b_forecast.py` on the same split and changes no other file. Per-product table: `price_errors.csv`.

## Setup and decisions

| Decision | Choice | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Split | train 232 + 241, test the new models of 242 (3728 products) | the forward test reported in `forecast_report.md`; `estimates.csv` is cross-validated across seasons and gives lower errors |
| Model | `gradient boosting, no analogues` | the chosen price model (landed cost x markup; no cost -> model without cost) |
| Worst | top 10% by absolute error, in € and in % (373 products each; 170 are in both) | errors in € weigh expensive products, errors in % weigh cheap ones |
| Small groups | groups under 25 test products pooled into `other (groups under 25)` | a share on a few products changes by tens of points with one product |
| Unusual markup | price / cost outside 0.8-1.25x the median markup of the category (training seasons) | describes the product after the fact (uses the actual price); not a model feature |
| Cost flags | `certain` / `suspect` from `phase5b_cost_check.flag_costs` | same rules as `cost_check.md` |

Overall on this split: MAE 2.645 €, MAPE 10.0%, exact price point
36.9%, within one price point 78.5%, bias -4.5%
(same numbers as `forecast_report.md`).

Column meaning in the tables below: `share of worst €` = of the worst products in €, the share in this group
(composition); `in worst € (rate)` = of this group's products, the share in the worst in € (expected
10% if errors were spread evenly). The same for %.

## Example: a product with a high cost and a high markup

`216630_BK` Shopper Bag MAXI DOTTS Black (sub-family Real Fur), validation split 232 -> 241 (it is a new model of 241).

| | value |
|---|---|
| list price | 120.00 € |
| landed cost | 30.94 € |
| actual markup (price / cost) | 3.88x |
| median markup of its sub-family in 232 | no product of this sub-family with a cost in 232 |
| median markup of its category in 232 | 3.30x (625 products) |
| markup estimated by the model | 2.34x (starting value 5.21x) |
| estimated price (chosen model) | 72.40 € |
| analogue estimate | 36.61 € (first analogues: 212925_HM, 212791_BK, 213191_BK, 211008_BK, 211435_BK) |

Largest SHAP contributions to the estimated markup, summed by feature family (negative = lowers the markup):

|  | log points | x markup |
|---|---|---|
| landed cost | -1.09 | 0.34 |
| attribute: GFS_DES_EN | 0.12 | 1.13 |
| weight | 0.12 | 1.13 |
| attribute: SUP_COD | 0.06 | 1.07 |
| number of sizes | -0.04 | 0.96 |
| attribute: CATEGORY_MATRIX | 0.03 | 1.03 |

Reading: the model starts from the average markup of its training rows and each family multiplies it by `x markup`. A large negative
contribution of `landed cost` means the model learned that products with a high cost tend to have a lower markup.
