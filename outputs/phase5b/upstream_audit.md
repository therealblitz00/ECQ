## Findings

### U01 (high, verified): Target

The target is what the buyer sold with the order and the allocation he chose, not demand. The planning classes (FASHIONTYPE, COLLECTION_TYPE: Capsule, Maxi, Commercial Online, Exclusive Online, Limited Edition...) are distribution programmes and carry most of the model (mean |SHAP| 0.49 and 0.20 in forecast_report.md, the two largest).

- **Evidence:** FASHIONTYPE alone explains 24% of the variance of log units inside a category, COLLECTION_TYPE 19%, CATEGORY_MATRIX 10%, SUP_COD 13% (PUR_BET 2%, TYPOLOGY 3%). Median log units: Capsule 4.9, Maxi 5.0, Commercial Online 5.3 against Fashion 7.9, Basic 8.3. Validation same-month ranking 0.475; without the two classes 0.342; adding the buyer's PUR_BET/TYPOLOGY 0.625. No stock, sell-through or store-count column exists, so a stock-out cannot be seen.
- **Bias:** Up: the ranking skill is skill at reproducing the buyer's plan; a product the buyer under-ordered looks like a bad product. Sales of a sold-out product are a floor of demand (censored).
- **Fix or check:** Say in the deliverable that the estimate is 'units the plan sells', not demand. Ask Parfois for stock, sell-through or weeks-to-sell-out per colourway (a censored target needs it). Check: refit with the classes removed and read it as the 'product-only' estimate; report both.

### U02 (high, verified): Target / time

Season 241 is called 'whole life' but its colourways had 15 to 53 weeks on sale at the extraction, and the model has no exposure feature (launch month was dropped). Units still grow with the time on sale.

- **Evidence:** Weeks on sale: 232 39-83 (median 64); 241 15-53 (median 38). Rank correlation of log units with weeks in 241: Apparel -0.08, Footwear -0.20, Hand Bag +0.29, Jewellery +0.26, Wallet +0.18. Correlation of the validation residual with weeks on sale: +0.17 overall, Apparel +0.09, Footwear -0.04, Hand Bag +0.28, Jewellery +0.23, Wallet +0.08. Only 28% of 241 had reached the outlet; 98% have a (later, planned) outlet date. Median realised price / list price (232 0.77, 241 0.83, 242 0.91) rises from the oldest to the newest season: longer lives include more markdown units, so a unit is not the same thing in 232 and 241.
- **Bias:** Mixed: training (232, ~64 weeks) and test (241, ~38 weeks) measure different horizons; products launched earlier in 241 are under-estimated (bias -25% for Nov-Dec 2023 launches in extraction_check.md).
- **Fix or check:** Check: refit on units normalised to a fixed horizon (units per week on sale, or only products with >= 26 weeks), and add weeks-to-extraction as a nuisance feature fixed to the horizon at prediction. Best fix: ask for weekly sales and use units in the first N weeks (full price only).

### U03 (medium, verified): Evaluation

The feature set was chosen with the 242 ranking after the validation and the test half both preferred a simpler one, and every forecast run re-looks at the same 241 data.

- **Evidence:** feature_selection.md: sales best on validation 'attributes only'; applied 'attributes + photo' (chosen against the validation winner: True). Test half also favours attributes only (same-month 0.508 vs 0.502, mae 0.514 vs 0.524). The report was regenerated 10 times and the script changed 9 times (git history); the CatBoost/metrics scripts add more looks. Test: 1142 models (1440 colourways), validation: 1153 models; both halves are in the same season, launch window and buyer team, so they are not independent.
- **Bias:** Up (mild): the reported test half is no longer untouched; differences of ~0.01 are inside the noise of the bootstrap.
- **Fix or check:** Freeze the protocol; treat the test half as spent. Confirm on a genuinely new sample (the 242 whole lives once they finish selling, same extraction rules). Prefer attributes-only (equal on val and test, no missing-photo problem).

### U04 (medium, verified): Data / deployment

Photos are missing for a quarter of season 242, but the model learned on seasons where only 2% are missing.

- **Evidence:** Share without a trusted photo: 232 2.0%, 241 1.5%, 242 24.6%. In 242 the products without a photo are the ones to forecast, and the photo components (16 numbers) are then missing: a pattern the trees never saw (validation error of the 26 such rows: 0.88 against 0.51).
- **Bias:** Unknown for 242 (the model falls back to its default branch for missing values); the validation cannot show it.
- **Fix or check:** Train with the photo components randomly blanked for 25% of rows, or use the attributes-only model for products without a photo (it loses nothing on validation or test). Report errors separately by photo availability.

### U05 (medium, verified): Evaluation / time

The 'same-month' metric groups by the planned launch month, but exposure follows the final date.

- **Evidence:** Planned and final store date differ for 56% of the colourways, by more than 30 days for 17%; the month differs for 35%; 88 planned dates are more than a year from the final one. Validation same-month ranking: 0.475 grouped by planned month, 0.486 by final month.
- **Bias:** The metric partly keeps the time effect it is meant to remove; the direction depends on the grouping.
- **Fix or check:** Report the same-month ranking with the final month too (it is only a grouping for the metric, not a feature), and flag planned dates far from the final one as data errors.

### U06 (medium, verified): Distribution shift

The seasons differ in the planning mix, so the estimate extrapolates to 242.

- **Evidence:** A classifier tells the 232 training rows from the new 241 models with AUC 0.90 on the tabular features (launch month excluded; net_weight 1894, price_pct_in_cat 778, price 550, CLR_TYPE 538), 0.88 without price and weight (CLR_TYPE 1819, n_sizes 954, SUP_COD 741, CLR_DES 665); 241 against 242: 0.89. Commercial Online goes from 1.9% (232) to 7.1% (242), Maxi from 5.7% to 0.6% (forecast_report.md); the planning classes drive the sales estimate (SHAP) and shift between seasons.
- **Bias:** Unknown sign; the sales level is not re-scaled between seasons (single 232 -> 241 transition, bias by category from -15% to +7%).
- **Fix or check:** Check: re-run the validation with the planning classes held to their 232 distribution (importance weights from the classifier) and look at the stability of the category-level bias.

### U07 (medium, suspected): Leakage (suspected)

CATEGORY_MATRIX and the planning classes may be revised after the first sales ('Sales below budget', 'Sales Over Budget' are recorded reclassification reasons).

- **Evidence:** 799 reclassified colourways (7.6%), 1383 with CATEGORY_MATRIX different from CATEGORY_MATRIX_BUYER (median log units 8.43 against 7.81). FASHIONTYPE_ASIS is identical to FASHIONTYPE, so no 'original' value exists to compare. The data do not say which field was revised or when.
- **Bias:** Up, if the classes were set after the sales were seen.
- **Fix or check:** Ask Parfois when each class is set (before the order or later) and which fields RECLASS_CODE changes. Check: refit without CATEGORY_MATRIX (leakage_checks.csv shows the three planning classes together give 0.399 vs 0.544 within category).

### U08 (low, verified): Target

Colourways that never sold are left out of the target as 'unknown', although for the finished seasons they are real zeros.

- **Evidence:** 39 colourways of 232/241 have no sales row, 39 of them launched before the extraction, plus 1 with 0 units: 0.6% of the 6,607 whole-life colourways. (The 331 without sales in 242 are launches after the extraction: correctly unknown.)
- **Bias:** Up for the sales level of low sellers by a small amount (survivorship); no effect on the ranking among sellers.
- **Fix or check:** Add a second, tiny model or a rule for 'will it sell at all' (flag items like those 40), or ask Parfois whether these were withdrawn before the shops. Report the share as a caveat.

### U09 (low, verified): Data / evaluation

Rare values of supplier, colour and category matrix: 12% to 20% of the validation products have a value with fewer than 20 training rows. Their error is slightly higher for SUP_COD and CLR_DES and not for CATEGORY_MATRIX; collapsing them does not help.

- **Evidence:** SUP_COD: 178 values, 260 validation rows in values with < 20 training rows, error 0.53 vs 0.50; CLR_DES: 100 values, 173 validation rows in values with < 20 training rows, error 0.55 vs 0.50; CATEGORY_MATRIX: 101 values, 297 validation rows in values with < 20 training rows, error 0.43 vs 0.53. Rare values grouped as OTHER (< 20 / < 50 rows): validation error 0.510 / 0.506 against 0.508.
- **Bias:** None measured (collapsing is neutral); the higher error on rare values is expected and already in the totals.
- **Fix or check:** Keep as is; give the buyer a flag 'supplier / colour seen < 20 times' next to the estimate, and a wider range for those.

### U10 (low, verified): Leakage (indirect)

The engine weights behind the 'similar past products' baseline and the price analogue were tuned with a sales objective that includes the 241 products.

- **Evidence:** phase4_eval.py: objective = mean(catalogue, visual, sales_corr), with `sales_pct_in_cat` of all seasons; the tuning half is a random half of the models of every season. The sales model itself does not use the weights (the analogue feature was not kept for sales); the price analogue uses them but never sees sales. Part of the 241 test models therefore sit in the tuning half.
- **Bias:** Down for the claimed gain of the model over the similar-products baseline (the baseline is slightly favoured).
- **Fix or check:** Low priority: re-tune the weights without `sales_corr` and check that the baseline numbers do not move.

### U11 (low, verified): Leakage (indirect)

Delivered cross-validated estimates use pools and ranks that include later seasons.

- **Evidence:** final_models builds the price analogues from every colourway with a price (242 included) and the sales/price features `price_pct_in_cat` is ranked across all seasons: the rank changes by more than 0.05 for 9% of the colourways when ranked inside the own season. No target of the test product is used, so the reported test numbers are not affected; the per-product estimates in estimates.csv are 'cross-validated', not 'as if launched at the time'.
- **Bias:** Up (slightly) for the delivered price estimates; none for the test metrics.
- **Fix or check:** Rank prices within the season of the product, and use a pool of earlier seasons only when you want an 'as at launch' estimate.

### U12 (low, verified): Leakage

Duplicates of a model across the split were checked and are not a problem.

- **Evidence:** Test colourways whose model is also in training: 0 (joining PROD_REF and PROD_REF_EQUIV) and 0 (PROD_REF only) of 1440; 10 re-coded rows. Photo near-duplicates (cosine > 0.97) between test and training: 2 of 1,417. Only 233 of 7,636 models exist in more than one season, and the test uses new models only.
- **Bias:** None.
- **Fix or check:** None. Note the test excludes carry-over models (149 known 241 colourways), so the numbers describe new models, not re-orders (ITEM_REPEAT).

### U13 (low, verified): Data quality

Landed cost is missing for a few colourways, mostly in some categories; MATERIAL exists for apparel only.

- **Evidence:** Missing landed cost: 175 colourways (Apparel 3.9%, Footwear 0.9%, Hand Bag 0.5%, Jewellery 1.5%, Wallet 0.4%); MATERIAL is empty for Apparel 16%, Footwear 100%, Hand Bag 100%, Jewellery 99%, Wallet 100% after the Phase 2a filling, and the feature is not in the chosen sales set anyway (SHAP 0.006).
- **Bias:** Small.
- **Fix or check:** Keep the fall-back; say in the report which categories rely on it.

### U14 (low, verified): Evaluation

The ranking metric rests on few groups.

- **Evidence:** Test: 1142 models, 1436 colourways in 47 category-month groups; only the 28 groups with more than 10 products are scored (1349 colourways, 94%); the rest are dropped without being reported. Colourways of a model are correlated, the bootstrap resamples models (good).
- **Bias:** None clear; wide intervals.
- **Fix or check:** Report how many groups and products the ranking uses.

## What was checked and found clean

- No negative or duplicated sales rows (10,185 rows, one per colourway); units and amounts are never below 0.
- Components of the photo and text embeddings are fitted on the training rows only (`pca`); the shuffled-target run gives ~0.
- No test colourway shares a model with training (U12); photo near-duplicates across the split are rare.
- Buyer decisions (PUR_BET, TYPOLOGY) are not features of the delivered sales model; they only appear in the reference row.

## Not tested here (needs data Parfois holds)

- Stock-outs and sell-through (no stock column); promotion calendars; store counts per product; units returned.
- Whether sales include several countries and currencies: `SALES_AMT_FX_RATE` is converted, but units are not split by market.
